01 / THE HUMAN BIT↙

About
Me.

Hopelessly inquisitive.
Occasionally coordinated.

I’m a data professional and program evaluation specialist with a focus on healthcare, research, and community initiatives. I care about what the numbers mean for the people behind them, especially when those people are too often left out of the conversation.

My work is about understanding what’s happening, asking what could be better, and helping turn that understanding into more equitable opportunities.

Outside of that? Soccer for 20+ years. A recurring identity crisis between powerlifting and bodybuilding. Boxing, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, sci-fi, true crime, poetry, spoken word, budget travel, and the occasional first-person shooter. Apparently, “one hobby” was a suggestion.
